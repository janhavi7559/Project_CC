Cybersecurity Awareness Survey

Please help us by filling out this short survey on cybersecurity for government institutions. Your responses will remain anonymous and are only used for educational purposes.

Summary of Survey Responses

  • Most common threats: Hacking & cyberattacks, Identity theft, Fake news & misinformation
  • Most trusted solution: Stronger laws & regulations (81%)
  • Awareness levels: Majority are somewhat to very aware of cybersecurity issues
  • Public trust: Most respondents partially trust their government to protect citizen data
  • Investment concern: Over 70% feel the government doesn't invest enough in cybersecurity
  • Suggestions: More employee training, AI-based monitoring, public awareness, stricter policies
